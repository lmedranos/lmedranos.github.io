SusML Workshop 2025

The SusML workshop was held at the Max Planck Institute for the Physics of Complex Systems in Dresden, Germany. It has been three intense and exciting days filled with inspiring talks and fruitful discussions on how to make machine learning research more sustainable. As always, many questions remain open, but I hope all participants enjoyed the workshop as much as I did. A big thanks to the co-organizers (Mariana Rossi, Gianaurelio Cuniberti. Milica Todorović, and Alexandre Tkatchenko) for their support in making this workshop possible!
